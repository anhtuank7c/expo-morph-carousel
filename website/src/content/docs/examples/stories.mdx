### How it works

1. Tap an avatar in the tray: a full-screen viewer opens on that person.
2. The people are the pages of an outer carousel with `pageEffect="cube"` and no media: swipe to the next person.
3. Each person's photos are an inner carousel with `mediaPosition="background"`, `mediaFit="cover"` and `liquid.preserveShape`: the rectangle stays sharp while the content melts.
4. The inner carousel has `gestureEnabled={false}`. A Pressable on top sends taps on the left third to `ref.prev()` and the rest to `ref.next()`; a long press pauses.
5. A Reanimated timing fills the progress bar of the current photo and calls `next()` at the end; the last photo hands over to the next person.
6. The tray prefetches every photo at the size the viewer will ask for (`usePrefetchImages` with `imageTargetFor`), so a story opens without waiting.
