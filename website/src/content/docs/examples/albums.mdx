### How it works

1. The covers are pages without media; `pageEffect={pageEffects.coverflow({ angle: 52, scale: 0.8, spacing: 0.56 })}` with a `pageSize` of 64% shows the neighbors turned away.
2. `positionValue` mirrors the position into a shared value: each blurred backdrop fades by its distance from it, on the UI thread.
3. With `loop` the position keeps counting; the backdrop compares on the circle.
4. The player buttons call `ref.prev()` and `ref.next()`; the end of a track skips to the next album.
