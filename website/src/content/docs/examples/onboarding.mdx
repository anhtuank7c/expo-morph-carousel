### How it works

1. `mediaPosition="background"` puts the media behind the pages; `mediaStyle` keeps it in the upper half and `pageStyle` pushes the text down.
2. `positionValue` drives the background tint with `interpolateColor` as the finger moves.
3. Next calls `ref.next()`; on the last page it closes the screen.
