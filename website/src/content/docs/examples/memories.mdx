### How it works

1. Full-screen photos (`mediaPosition="background"`, `mediaFit="cover"`) with a slow, wide liquid melt (`liquid={{ preserveShape: true, blur: 40 }}`, `animation={{ type: 'timing', duration: 900 }}`).
2. The carousel sits in an Animated.View that zooms in on one photo and out on the next (Ken Burns), so a page change never jumps.
3. A timing fills the progress bar and calls `ref.next()`; with `loop` the trip starts over.
4. `tapToNavigate` goes back on the left side and forward elsewhere, like stories.
