### How it works

1. PNGs on a transparent background are what the liquid effect is made for: the blob takes the outline of one product and reforms as the next.
2. `mediaHeight` and `mediaPadding` give the products room; `mediaFit` is `contain` by default.
3. `getPlaceholderColor` shows a soft blob of the product's color while it loads.
4. `renderFooter` gets the current item: the button takes its color.
