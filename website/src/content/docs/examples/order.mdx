### How it works

1. The photos share one square frame and stand on its bottom edge, so they keep their real proportions and the liquid stretches from tall to wide.
2. The `Pagination` slot is the tab bar: a pill moves with `position` and takes each item's color with `interpolateColor`.
3. The `MediaBackground` slot draws a glow in the item's tint and a floor shadow whose width follows the item.
4. `renderFooter` adds the current item to a small cart kept in state.
