### How it works

1. `direction="vertical"` pages up and down; each post is a full-screen page over its photo (`mediaPosition="background"`).
2. The photo URL is built for the area the carousel measures (`getImage(item, index, target)`), so it is never larger than needed.
3. Double tap anywhere to like: two taps within 280 ms play a heart burst.
4. While a post is in view (`index === current` from `onIndexChange`) the record turns and the progress bar fills.
5. The chip at the top switches `effect` between `liquid`, `slide` and `parallax` on the same carousel.
