### How it works

1. The banner loops and autoplays (`loop`, `autoplay={{ interval: 3500 }}`); photos melt with `liquid.preserveShape`. `footerStyle` lays the dots over the photo.
2. The deal row is a card carousel without media: `pageSize` 44% of the width, `pageAlign="start"` and `pageGap` show the next cards, `snapMode="momentum"` glides over several with a fling.
3. Each card is plain React Native: image, discount badge, price and a "sold" bar.
4. The deal of the day puts the product photo in the media area (`mediaHeight`, `mediaFit="cover"`), the name and price slide underneath and `renderFooter` holds the Buy button in the item's color.
